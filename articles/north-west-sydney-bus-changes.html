Transport update

North West Sydney bus changes from 21 June 2026

Transport for NSW has released a summary of bus changes across Region 4 and 7, including a new 24-hour Victoria Road route, several withdrawn services, and more Metro feeder improvements.

June 2026 • North Sydney Transport Association

Date

New route

  • Route 52 introduced between Parramatta and City Hyde Park via Victoria Road.
  • Route 52 joins the All-Day Frequent Network.
  • Services will run every 10 minutes or better during the day.
  • Services will run every 20 minutes or better early morning and late evening.
  • Route 52 will operate 24 hours a day.
  • Overnight services will run every 30 minutes.

Routes withdrawn

  • 500N Parramatta to City Hyde Park via Victoria Road withdrawn.
  • 500X West Ryde to City Hyde Park via Victoria Road withdrawn.
  • 552 Oatlands to Parramatta withdrawn.

Major service improvements

  • 521 Parramatta to Eastwood via Park Road: more services, 30-minute frequency across the full route, and extended operating hours.
  • 523 West Ryde to Parramatta via Bartlett Street: more services, extended hours, and rerouting via South Street, Rydalmere.
  • 524 Ryde and West Ryde to Parramatta via Melrose Park: more services, extended hours, and rerouting directly along Victoria Road, Rydalmere.
  • 525 Parramatta to Sydney Olympic Park via Newington: evening frequency improved from every 60 minutes to every 30 minutes, with extended operating hours.
  • 549 Parramatta to Epping via North Rocks: more peak services to improve connections to M1 Metro services at Epping.
  • 550 Parramatta to Macquarie Park via Epping: increased frequency.
  • 605 North Kellyville to Rouse Hill: more peak services to improve connections to M1 Metro services at Rouse Hill.
  • 626 Kellyville to Pennant Hills via Cherrybrook: increased frequency around Cherrybrook Station, including approximately every 8 minutes in the AM peak and every 10 minutes in the PM peak.
  • 637 Glenorie to Castle Hill via Galston and Round Corner: one additional weekday evening trip.
  • 642X Dural to City Wynyard: increased frequency between Cherrybrook Station and Dural Round Corner on weekdays.
  • 643 Gables to Rouse Hill via Box Hill: more peak services and longer operating hours.

Route changes

  • 501 Parramatta to Central Pitt Street via Victoria Road and Pyrmont will change to operate between Central and West Ryde only.
  • 501 will also be adjusted to replace route 500X between Top Ryde and West Ryde.
  • 525 will operate between Parramatta and Lidcombe, providing a new connection from Newington to Lidcombe.
  • 525 will no longer operate to Sydney Olympic Park.
  • 546 Parramatta to Epping via Oatlands and Carlingford will have minor path changes in North Parramatta.
  • 546 services currently starting or ending at Carlingford Court will extend to Carlingford Light Rail.
  • 553 Beecroft to West Pennant Hills loop service will no longer operate selected trips via Range Road.
  • 608 Windsor to Rouse Hill will no longer divert to Arndell College in the afternoon. New school route 3608 will operate instead.
  • 615X North Kellyville to City QVB will no longer operate between North Kellyville Square and Stringer Road.
  • 620X Cherrybrook to City Wynyard will no longer operate between Purchase Road and Macquarie Drive.
  • 622 Dural to Milsons Point via Cherrybrook will no longer operate between James Henty Drive, Dural and Macquarie Drive, Cherrybrook.
  • Some 642X trips to and from the Sydney CBD will now start and finish at Cherrybrook Station.

North Shore changes

The following North Shore and Lower North Shore routes receive timetable adjustments to improve reliability and better meet passenger demand:

  • 113 Chatswood to Royal North Shore Hospital
  • 115 Chatswood to North Sydney Station loop service
  • 119 Gore Hill to North Sydney Station loop service
  • 120 Chatswood to City QVB loop service, with timetable adjustments and more services
  • 203 Castlecrag to North Sydney
  • 206 East Lindfield to City Bridge Street via Freeway
  • 251 Riverview to McMahons Point via North Sydney
  • 253 Riverview to City Wynyard via Freeway
  • 254 Riverview to McMahons Point via North Sydney
  • 269 North Sydney to Milsons Point via Kirribilli loop service
  • 286 Denistone East to Milsons Point via St Leonards and North Sydney
  • 291 Epping to McMahons Point via North Sydney
  • 297 Denistone East to City Wynyard via Lane Cove Tunnel
  • 536 Gladesville to Chatswood via Hunters Hill

Metro feeder changes

A clear theme of the changes is stronger integration with Sydney Metro, especially at Epping, Rouse Hill and Cherrybrook.

  • 549 gains more peak services for Epping Metro connections.
  • 605 gains more peak services for Rouse Hill Metro connections.
  • 626 receives a major frequency boost around Cherrybrook Station.
  • 642X gains more services between Cherrybrook Station and Dural Round Corner.
  • Some long-distance CBD express services are shortened, with passengers directed toward Metro connections.

Overall summary

The 21 June 2026 changes introduce a major new frequent Victoria Road route, withdraw some duplicative services, improve several local routes, and continue the shift toward Metro-focused feeder networks in North West Sydney.

For North Sydney and the Lower North Shore, the changes are mostly timetable adjustments rather than major route restructures.
