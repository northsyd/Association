The NSTA Community

Transport is better when we do it together.

NSTA brings together students, alumni and transport enthusiasts with an interest in public transport, transport history, urban planning, infrastructure and the future of our cities.

Our community provides a place to discuss transport, share ideas, participate in Association activities and stay connected with what we're working on.

Current Arrangements

Membership is still taking shape.

NSTA is currently developing its formal membership arrangements. For now, our Discord community provides the main way for members and supporters to connect with the Association.

Further information about formal membership arrangements will be published here as they are established.
