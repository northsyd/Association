Heritage Bus Shelters

Overview

About the project

NSTA advocated for the renewal of heritage bus shelters as part of the Neutral Bay–Mosman transport discussion, while highlighting the importance of useful passenger information at stops.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

Heritage infrastructure should be preserved where practicable while still providing passengers with modern, clear and accessible transport information.

Progress

Our work

  • Advocated for the renewal of heritage bus shelters.
  • Called for improved passenger information and timetable information.
  • Raised the importance of transport wayfinding within the project.
