About the project

The Newcastle Region Commission examines transport issues across the Newcastle region, bringing together research, public discussion and advocacy on the future of transport in the area.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA supports evidence-based transport planning that improves connectivity, safety, capacity and long-term network resilience across the Newcastle region.

Progress

Our work

  • Conducting research into regional transport issues.
  • Monitoring developments affecting the Newcastle transport network.
  • Developing advocacy positions and recommendations.
  • Engaging with relevant public discussion and stakeholders.
