Overview

About the project

More Than Buses is NSTA's broader transport strategy within the Northern Beaches Network Review. It considers how the region can develop a more diverse and resilient public transport network rather than relying on buses alone.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA supports a long-term transport network that combines buses with higher-capacity and higher-quality modes where demand and geography justify them.

Progress

Our work

  • Developed a long-term light rail and corridor strategy.
  • Examined transport opportunities across the Lower North Shore and Northern Beaches.
  • Developed recommendations for the Northern Beaches Network Review.
  • Considered future network resilience, capacity and connectivity.
