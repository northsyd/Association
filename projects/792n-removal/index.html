792N Removal

Project Status

Active

Project Lead

Lachlan Steenson

Overview

About the project

NSTA is campaigning for the permanent withdrawal of the 792N school bus service between North Sydney Boys High School and Mosman. The campaign is based on observed low patronage and the view that school transport resources should be directed toward services that better meet passenger demand.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA supports reviewing and withdrawing services where persistent low patronage means the existing arrangement is not delivering an effective use of transport resources. The Association is also advocating for more useful school transport options.

Progress

Our work

  • Observed and documented patronage on the 792N.
  • Submitted feedback to Transport for NSW.
  • Prepared correspondence for the Minister for Transport.
  • Engaged with elected representatives and transport stakeholders.
  • Raised the issue publicly through NSTA's advocacy campaign.
