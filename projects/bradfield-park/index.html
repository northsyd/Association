Overview

About the project

NSTA is examining the proposed use of Bradfield Park South, including its relationship with the Sydney Harbour Bridge and surrounding transport infrastructure.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA is focused on ensuring transport access, accessibility, public space and community considerations are properly examined before decisions are made about the site's future use.

Progress

Our work

  • Established an inquiry into the proposed use of Bradfield Park South.
  • Examined transport and accessibility implications.
  • Considered the site's relationship with surrounding road and public transport infrastructure.
  • Developed public advocacy around the future of the site.
