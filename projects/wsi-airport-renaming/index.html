Project Lead

Overview

About the project

NSTA is examining the proposed name of the railway station serving Western Sydney International Airport and advocating for a naming outcome that is clear, useful to passengers and consistent with the wider transport network.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

Station naming should provide passengers with an immediate and unambiguous understanding of the destination served, while fitting logically within the wider Western Sydney transport network.

Progress

Our work

  • Developing NSTA's position on the proposed station name.
  • Researching naming and wayfinding considerations.
  • Preparing advocacy material for the relevant decision-makers.
