Heritage Bus Shelters

Advocating for the renewal of North Sydney's heritage bus shelters while ensuring passenger information remains part of the design.

Advocacy Outcome

A better future for North Sydney's heritage shelters.

NSTA supported the renewal of North Sydney's heritage bus shelters and called for passenger information to be incorporated into the renewed shelters.

In August 2026, North Sydney Council unanimously supported the renewal of the heritage bus shelters and recommended that transport and timetabling information be incorporated into the shelter designs.

View project →
The Outcome

From advocacy to action.

The Council's unanimous support marked a significant outcome for the project and reflected NSTA's call for transport information to be considered alongside the physical renewal of the shelters.

The project demonstrates the role young people can play in contributing to local transport and infrastructure decisions.
