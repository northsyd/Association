Overview

About the project

South-East Studies is a research portfolio rather than a single campaign. It provides a framework for investigating transport networks, infrastructure and planning issues across Sydney's south-east.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA's research will focus on practical transport improvements, network connectivity and long-term planning outcomes.

Progress

Our work

  • Developing a research program for the region.
  • Identifying transport issues suitable for further study.
  • Preparing future research and advocacy projects.
