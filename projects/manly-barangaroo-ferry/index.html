Project Lead

Overview

About the project

This project examines the role of direct ferry connectivity between Manly and Barangaroo and the potential benefits of improving connections between the Northern Beaches and Sydney's CBD.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA is examining how ferry services can provide useful, reliable and well-integrated connections between the Northern Beaches and the wider Sydney transport network.

Progress

Our work

  • Developing NSTA's position on the proposed service.
  • Examining network connectivity and interchange opportunities.
  • Considering the role of ferry services within the broader Northern Beaches transport network.
