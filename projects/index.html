Our Projects

What We Do

From campaigns to long-term transport planning.

NSTA projects cover a broad range of transport issues, from local campaigns and infrastructure advocacy to research, network planning and engagement with decision-makers.

Projects are managed by the Association and may involve research, submissions, public advocacy, community engagement or collaboration with external organisations.
