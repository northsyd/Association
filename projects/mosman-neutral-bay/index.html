Overview

About the project

NSTA is engaging with the proposed Neutral Bay–Mosman corridor upgrade, with a focus on transport outcomes, accessibility, passenger information and the role of young transport users in project planning.

NSTA's Position

What we believe

NSTA supports transport improvements that make the corridor safer, more accessible and easier to use, while ensuring public transport users remain part of the project's stakeholder process.

Progress

Our work

  • Engaged with North Sydney Council regarding the project.
  • Advocated for public transport and accessibility outcomes.
  • Participated in the public discussion surrounding the corridor upgrade.
  • Secured recognition of NSTA as a stakeholder alongside transport service operators.
