Our Governing Document

The foundation of NSTA.

The NSTA Constitution establishes the purpose, structure and governance of the North Sydney Transport Association.

It sets out the organisation's offices, responsibilities, membership arrangements and procedures for its operation.

What It Covers

The rules behind the organisation.

Article I

Organisation & Purpose

Establishes the Association and sets out its purpose and organisational identity.

Article II

Membership

Sets out the framework for membership within the Association.

Article III

Executive

Establishes the Executive and the offices responsible for administering the Association.

Article IV

Governance & Procedure

Provides the framework for organisational decision-making and internal procedures.

Amendments

Keeping the Constitution current.

Amendments to the Constitution are recorded as part of NSTA's governance process.

Where amendments are formally adopted, updated versions of the Constitution will be published here.
