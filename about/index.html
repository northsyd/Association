About NSTA

Who We Are

A youth-led organisation advocating for better transport across Sydney and beyond.

Our organisation

Young people have a stake in transport.

The North Sydney Transport Association (NSTA) is a youth-led transport advocacy organisation working to improve public transport and transport outcomes for our communities.

We bring together young people who care about transport and give them a platform to research, discuss and advocate for the changes they want to see.

Our work spans public transport advocacy, transport proposals, community engagement, submissions and campaigns.

What We Do

Advocacy with a purpose.

Our work takes a number of different forms, depending on the transport issue we're tackling.

Research

We investigate

We examine transport networks, services and proposals to understand what's working and where improvements are needed.

Advocacy

We advocate

We put forward practical ideas and campaign for changes with governments, councils and transport decision-makers.

Engagement

We engage

We work to make sure young transport users have a voice in conversations about the future of their networks.

Our Approach

Practical. Independent. Youth-led.

NSTA is focused on transport. We aim to keep our advocacy constructive, informed and focused on achievable outcomes.

We believe transport decisions should consider the experiences of the people who actually use the network — including young people.

We're independent and develop our own positions on the issues we campaign on.
