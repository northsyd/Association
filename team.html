02 / Representation

A network beyond
North Sydney.

NSTA brings together young people interested in transport from across Australia.

NSTA includes members and delegates from 14 schools across New South Wales and Western Australia, with additional representation from Queensland.

Perth inset

Queensland is represented within NSTA through an interstate delegate.
