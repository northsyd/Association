Introduction & About

Student-led transport advocacy for a better connected North Sydney.

The North Sydney Transport Association researches, discusses and advocates for better transport outcomes across North Sydney and the wider Sydney network.

What we stand for

Better public transport, better streets, better planning.

NSTA turns student interest in transport into submissions, proposals, excursions and public advocacy.

What we do

Our work

Submissions

We write formal responses to council and government consultations on transport planning.

Projects

We develop ideas for corridors, stations, bus priority, rail links and active transport.

Events

We run debates, excursions, discussions and transport-themed activities.

Media

We publish updates, statements and explainers on local transport issues.

FAQ

Common questions

What is NSTA?

NSTA is an independent student-led transport advocacy association focused on North Sydney and Sydney transport issues.

Are you part of Transport for NSW or council?

No. NSTA is independent unless a partnership or affiliation is clearly stated.

What do members actually do?

Members can help with research, writing, graphics, events, field trips, photography and public engagement.

Can non-experts join?

Yes. Interest and willingness to contribute matter more than technical knowledge.
