02 / FAQ

Common questions

New to NSTA? Here's what you need to know.

What does NSTA actually do? +

NSTA advocates for better public transport, streets and connections. We participate in consultations, develop proposals, engage with decision-makers, publish research and commentary, and run community and youth-focused activities.

Who can get involved? +

NSTA is youth-led, with opportunities for young people to contribute to advocacy, research, media, events, graphics, photography and other projects.

Do I need to be a transport expert? +

Not at all. An interest in transport and a willingness to contribute are more important than technical knowledge. There are plenty of ways to get involved without being a transport planner or engineer.

Is NSTA part of the government? +

No. NSTA is an independent advocacy organisation. We are not part of Transport for NSW, North Sydney Council or any school unless a specific partnership or affiliation is clearly stated.

Where does NSTA operate? +

Our work is centred on North Sydney and the wider Sydney transport network, while our advocacy can extend to transport issues across NSW.

How can I support an NSTA campaign? +

Follow our updates, share our campaigns, contribute your perspective, attend relevant consultations or events, and encourage decision-makers to consider the issues we're raising.

Can organisations work with NSTA? +

Yes. We welcome appropriate opportunities to collaborate with community organisations, youth groups and other organisations on transport-related initiatives.

How can I contact NSTA? +

You can contact us at nsta@northsydneytransport.org or connect with us through Instagram or Discord.
