About NSTA

Young people should have a say in transport.

NSTA is a youth-led transport advocacy organisation focused on improving public transport through research, community advocacy, submissions and engagement with decision-makers.

We believe the people who use transport every day should have a meaningful voice in how it is planned and delivered.
